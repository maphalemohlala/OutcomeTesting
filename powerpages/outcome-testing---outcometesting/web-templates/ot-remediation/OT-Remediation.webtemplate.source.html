Working-day ageing against the BR-010 ten-day threshold, per OD-018 (resolved 2026-08-30): Monday to Friday, the day a case enters remediation counts as day 1, and England's bank holidays are applied manually rather than encoded - so a period spanning Christmas or Easter reads older here than it truly is and a breach near a bank holiday wants a human look. The count runs in the browser rather than in Liquid because Liquid has no date arithmetic. It is fed calendar dates, not timestamps, which is what keeps it free of the timezone question app/src/lib/workingDays.ts has to solve: the server has already resolved which UK day each value falls on.
