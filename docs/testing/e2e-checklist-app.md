| APP-024 | Case detail | Upload at 23:30 UK time and check the due date. | The deadline is counted from the UK day, not the UTC instant. | Due date UK-day rule | Pass on the rule, with a correction to the row's own premise. **At 23:30 UK time the two days never differ** - the UK runs at UTC or UTC+1, so its clock is never behind, and 23:30 in London is 22:30 or 23:30 UTC on the same date.
